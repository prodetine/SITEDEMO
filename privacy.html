PRIVACY INFORMATION

This development version prepares a project brief in your browser. It does not submit form entries to a server, store them in local storage, or report that an enquiry has been delivered.

Your project brief

Your name, email address and project description remain in the page until you choose to copy or share the prepared message. Reloading the page clears the prepared message; your browser may independently restore form fields.

Email and Telegram

When a contact destination is configured, you may open an email draft or a Telegram chat. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Opening an email draft passes the prepared message to your email application; you choose whether to send it. A Telegram link opens the configured chat; you copy and paste the message yourself.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not install analytics, advertising trackers or application cookies. Fonts and website images are served with the website. A hosting provider may process request logs such as IP addresses according to its own policies.

External links

Links to other websites open third-party services. Their operators are responsible for their privacy practices.

Before production launch

The studio’s legal identity and privacy contact have not yet been configured. This development notice must be completed with the operator’s details and actual data-handling practices before online lead collection is introduced.
